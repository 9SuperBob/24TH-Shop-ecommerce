# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shoppers browse a small product catalog, inspect items, and manage a cart before checkout. They need to recognize featured product categories quickly and navigate the store confidently on both desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

This React storefront demonstrates product discovery and cart management with data from a product API. Success means that shoppers can find products, view details, and update a cart with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, and welcoming. The interface should make the catalog feel approachable without competing with product imagery.

## Anti-references

Avoid over-decorated commerce templates, autoplay motion that distracts from shopping, low-contrast text, and controls that are difficult to use on touch devices.

## Design Principles

1. Let product imagery lead product discovery.
2. Keep navigation and shopping actions familiar.
3. Make every interactive control clear, reachable, and keyboard-accessible.
4. Preserve a calm reading experience by using motion only for meaningful changes.
5. Adapt layouts structurally for smaller screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide meaningful image descriptions, and ensure all slider controls have accessible labels.
